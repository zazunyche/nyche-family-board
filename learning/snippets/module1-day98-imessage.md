# AI Agents Day 98: Context rot — lost in the middle

*Module 1.3, Day 98 | iMessage Edition*

---

**The concept:** Yesterday was compaction — what happens when the whiteboard *fills up*. Today's problem is sneakier: even when everything still fits, I don't attend to it evenly. Models reliably pay most attention to the **start** and the **end** of a long window, and least to the **middle**. The info is right there, uncompacted — but a fact buried mid-context gets quietly under-weighted. As the window grows, that dilution gets worse. The name for it is **context rot**.

**Concrete:** Put a key constraint in message 3 of 50, and another in message 48, and I'll likely honor both. Put the same constraint at message 25 and I may skim past it — not because it's gone, but because my attention thinned in the middle.

**Analogy:** Reading a 30-page contract. You remember clause 1 and the signature line; clause 14 blurs. You *can* see it — your attention just didn't hold there.

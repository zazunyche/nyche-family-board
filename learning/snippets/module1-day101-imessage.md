# AI Agents Day 101: When the Whiteboard Fills Up

*Module 1.3, Day 101 | iMessage Edition*

---

**The concept:** As a conversation or a long task runs, my context window — the whiteboard — fills up. Two things go wrong. It can *overflow* (no room left). And even before that, the middle gets fuzzy: models attend best to what's at the *start* and the *end*, so a fact buried in the middle of a very full window can get quietly overlooked. The fix is **compaction** — I periodically summarize the old middle into a compact note, drop the raw detail, and keep the window lean and sharp.

**Concrete:** In a long planning session I don't carry every message word-for-word. I compress "here's what we've settled" into a short summary, free up the board, and the recent, important stuff stays crisp instead of drowning.

**Analogy:** A whiteboard filling up mid-meeting. You photograph it, wipe it, and rewrite just the conclusions — so there's room to keep working and the key points stay legible.

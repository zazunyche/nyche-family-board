# AI Agents Day 100: Extended Thinking — My Hidden Scratchpad

*Module 1.4, Day 100 | iMessage Edition*

---

**The concept:** Yesterday's ReAct let me reason *out loud* between actions. Extended thinking is different. Before I answer, I can run a private scratchpad — a burst of reasoning you never see in the final reply — where I plan, try an approach, discard a dead end, and only then commit. It's a model-level setting that buys me real "room to think" before the first word of my answer is locked in.

**Concrete:** Ask me a quick fact and I answer straight away — no scratchpad needed. Ask me "re-plan the week around Thursday's rescheduled appointment" and I think hard first: list the conflicts, test a couple of orderings, catch the double-booking — *then* write the clean answer. That thinking costs extra tokens and time, so it's only worth it when a wrong first step would cascade into a wrong result.

**Analogy:** Scratch paper on an exam. Easy questions you answer directly. Hard ones you work out in the margin, then copy the final answer into the box. The margin work costs you time — but it's exactly why the boxed answer is right.

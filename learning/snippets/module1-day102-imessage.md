# AI Agents Day 102: Plan Ahead, or Feel Your Way?

*Module 1.4, Day 102 | iMessage Edition*

---

**The concept:** When you hand me a task I have two ways to approach it. I can **plan upfront** — map the whole sequence of steps before I touch anything — or I can **react** — take one step, look at what actually happened, decide the next step from there. Planning shines when the world is stable and the steps depend on each other. Reacting shines when the world is uncertain and each result changes what I should do next. The art is knowing which — and most real tasks are a blend: a rough plan, then react as reality pushes back.

**Concrete:** "Book the usual table for Friday" — I plan: check calendar, call, confirm. Clean, predictable. But "sort out the catering for Dad's thing" — I don't over-plan step 9 before step 1, because what the venue says will rewrite everything after it. I plan loosely and adjust.

**Analogy:** A road trip. Map the route before you leave — but when there's a crash on I-95, you don't re-run the whole plan. You reroute from where you are.

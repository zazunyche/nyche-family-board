**The tradeoff:** Not every task needs your smartest, most expensive model. Claude comes in tiers — Haiku (fast, cheap), Sonnet (balanced), Opus (powerful), Fable 5 (maximum capability). The rule: use the cheapest model that reliably completes the task.

**In Earnventory:** Use Haiku to classify whether an invoice is past-due (simple yes/no, high volume). Use Sonnet to draft a supplier follow-up email (moderate judgment required). Use Opus to audit your full purchasing history and recommend margin optimization across 500 SKUs (complex reasoning over large context). Haiku costs $1/million input tokens; Opus costs $5/million — a 5× difference that compounds fast at scale.

**Analogy:** Think of it like hiring contractors. You don't fly in a $500/hour specialist to fill out a form. Haiku is your reliable generalist for high-volume routine work. Sonnet handles most real business tasks well. Opus is the specialist you call for genuinely hard problems.

# AI Agents Day 107: ReAct — The Loop That Makes Agents Smart

*Module 1.4, Day 107 | iMessage Edition*

---

**The concept:** Yesterday's chain of thought was pure thinking — I reason, then answer, all in my own head. But I can't *know* whether a supplier emailed you by thinking harder; that fact lives in the world, not in me. **ReAct** fixes that: I interleave reasoning with *actions*. Reason ("I should check the inbox") → Act (call the email tool) → Observe (read what comes back) → Reason again ("two invoices, both flagged urgent") → Act… and repeat until done. Thought steers the next action; the observation steers the next thought.

**Concrete:** "Any supplier invoices this week?" I don't guess. I reason I need the inbox, call the tool, read the three results, reason about which are invoices, maybe open one to confirm, then summarize. Each loop is grounded in real data.

**Analogy:** It's the scientific method, once per turn — hypothesize, run the experiment, read the result, revise. Thinking plus checking reality, over and over.

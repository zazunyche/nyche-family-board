AI Agents Day 76: Zazu and Abeiku — A Live Case Study

**Concept:** You've seen orchestrator/subagent in abstract — now trace it through a system you actually run. When Zazu delegates to Abeiku, it doesn't share its full context. It composes a narrow brief: the task, the learner profile, the format, the quality bar. Abeiku spawns with that brief as its entire starting context. It never sees your family's MEMORY.md, Zazu's conversation history, or any other household state. It does the work, returns a deliverable, and Zazu synthesizes that result back into a response to you. The boundary between agents is sharp: one brief goes in, one finished artifact comes out.

**Example:** This curriculum. Zazu briefed Abeiku on subject area, Dad's background (MIT CS, Earnventory, familiar with Zazu), 50-day format, and quality bar. Abeiku got no family schedules, no unread emails, no other open tasks. The isolation was the point — a clean context window for a long research job.

**Analogy:** Hiring a specialist consultant project by project. They get the brief, not a badge to your whole office.

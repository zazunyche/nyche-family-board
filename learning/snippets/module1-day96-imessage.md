# AI Agents Day 96: Chain of Thought — Showing My Work

*Module 1.4, Day 96 | iMessage Edition*

---

**The concept:** We open Mini-Module 1.4 — Reasoning. Here's the surprising part: when I "think step by step," I'm not thinking privately and then reporting. I'm literally writing the steps out, and each step I write becomes part of what I read to produce the next one. The reasoning is load-bearing text, not commentary. That's why one instruction — "show your work" — measurably raises my accuracy on anything with more than one operation.

**Concrete:** Ask me a bare "What's the landed cost of a $42 SKU with a 17% tariff?" and I may blurt a number. Ask me to work it out and I write "17% of 42 = 7.14; 42 + 7.14 = 49.14" — and the $49.14 is now pinned to a visible calculation you can check. If I'm wrong, you see *where*.

**Analogy:** It's showing your work on a math test. Not for the teacher's benefit — each written line keeps the next line honest about what came before.
